Privacy

This site does not track you

It uses no cookies, no trackers, no analytics, and no fonts or resources loaded from a third-party service. It contains no forms.

Host logs

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub may record technical connection data (IP address, browser) in its logs. We have no access to them. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Writing to us

If you write to purenova3d@free.fr, we use your address only to reply and keep it for the duration of the exchange. You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by writing to the same address. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (French data protection authority).

The LithoShape3D software

Calculations run on your computer: your photos are never sent over the internet. Only license activation contacts the license server (license key and device identifier).
